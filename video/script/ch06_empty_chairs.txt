And then the world gave millions of people a reason to need that doorway.

World War One kills more than a hundred and sixteen thousand Americans. Then the 1918 flu kills around six hundred seventy-five thousand more. Most of the dead are young. They leave behind parents. Wives. Sweethearts.

So, just like after the Civil War, Spiritualism comes roaring back. Even Arthur Conan Doyle, the man who invented Sherlock Holmes, the most logical detective in fiction, spends his last years preaching that the dead are still with us.

And Ouija boards sell like never before. Mothers and wives buy them to reach sons and husbands who aren't coming home.

So picture one house around 1920. In the parlor, a teenager and her date, knees touching, giggling over the board. Upstairs, her mother, with the same kind of board, trying to reach the son she lost in France.

Same object. Two completely different stories.
