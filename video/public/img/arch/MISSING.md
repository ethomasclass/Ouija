# Archival images: missing or partial

## Not found

### william_fuld: portrait of William Fuld (pre-1931)
I could not find a portrait that is clearly public domain. Searches tried:
- Library of Congress photos for "William Fuld". Only Fuld's 1902 trademark registrations came up (Oracle, "Did U. C. Billie?"), and neither has a portrait.
- Wikimedia Commons for "William Fuld", "Fuld Ouija" and "William Fuld portrait". Only the NARA patent case file for patent 1,125,833 (talking boards) came up: document pages, no portrait.
- Chronicling America for "William Fuld" ouija, 1900–1930, and for Fuld + ouija, 1921–1930 and 1927 (the year he died). I checked the July 4, 1920 Evening Star and Washington Times articles about Fuld. Neither has a photo. I saved the Evening Star headline as `fuld_article_1920.jpg` instead. I found no 1927 obituary with a photo; the Baltimore Sun is not in Chronicling America.
- Ideas for later: Baltimore Sun or Baltimore American (Feb. 1927) via ProQuest or newspapers.com. Check rights carefully; any pre-1931 publication is fine. The Fuld family photos on museumoftalkingboards.com and williamfuld.com have unclear rights, so I did not use them.

## Partial or caveats

- **pearl_curran (1910s)**: The Commons image `pearl_curran.jpg` comes from W. F. Prince's *The Case of Patience Worth* (1927). It is low resolution (491x614), and the photo probably dates from the 1920s, not the 1910s. A better, higher-resolution option dated 1920 is `pearl_curran_1920.jpg` (Washington Times, Jan. 25, 1920). I found no 1910s photo of Curran in the LOC photo collections (a "Pearl Curran" search returned nothing relevant). The only Internet Archive copies of the 1927 Prince book are 1964 reprints with restricted lending.
- **ad_pittsburgh_1891**: The best match is Danziger's ad in the Pittsburg Dispatch, Feb. 1, 1891, p. 12: "OUIJA, or, The Wonderful Talking Board ... marvelous accuracy". The printed price is **$1.49**, not $1.50. Other 1891 Dispatch hits were Feb. 8 p. 14, Feb. 15 p. 5, Mar. 1 p. 12, Mar. 8 p. 14 and Mar. 23 p. 5. The Feb. 8 hit is only a one-line mention at the foot of another Danziger's ad, so I did not save it. I did not crop the other hits.
- **rockwell_ouija_1920**: The full cover scan comes from Internet Archive microfilm, so it is black and white (1838x2472). A color reproduction of the painting (no masthead) from Commons is only 714x822 and is saved as `rockwell_ouija_1920_color.jpg`. I found no higher-resolution color cover scan with clear rights.
- **faraday_table_1853**: The Illustrated London News engraving of Faraday's apparatus is 1069x963 (Commons crop from microfilm). The Internet Archive ILN 1853-07-16 issue's page list did not include p. 35, so I did not pull a higher-resolution copy.
- **flu_masks_1918_seattle_police**: Only a 1920 px Commons rendition is saved. upload.wikimedia.org kept returning HTTP 429 for the 3762 px original. The original is also at NARA, 165-WW-269B-25.
- **wwi_soldiers, flu_ward_1918, ouija_box_1920**: These are 1536 px wide. That is the full size of the LOC master TIFF for these items (copy negatives).
